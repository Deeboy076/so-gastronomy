ABOUT SOUL FOOD

FOOD WITH SOUL.

Located in the heart of Yaba, Soul Food Restaurant brings together comforting flavours, generous portions and a welcoming atmosphere designed for everyday dining and special moments alike.

WHY PEOPLE STOP IN

Warm meals. Easy vibes. Great company.

Soul Food is a comfort-first dining destination for busy Lagos days, casual lunches, dinner dates, family catch-ups and relaxed nights out with friends. The menu brings together African favourites, local comfort, and a few international classics for different tastes and different kinds of occasions.

Whether you want to dine in, grab takeaway, or have your favourites delivered, the experience is designed to feel relaxed, welcoming and genuinely easy. Breakfast, lunch and dinner are served in a space built for conversation, laughter and lingering a little longer than planned.
